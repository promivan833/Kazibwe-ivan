HELP & INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

A few practical answers before starting a project.

What types of websites do you create?

I work on personal websites, portfolios and websites for small businesses. The exact pages and features are agreed before development starts.

Will my website work on phones?

I aim to build responsive layouts that adapt to smartphones, tablets and desktop screens. The finished pages should be checked on the devices and browsers relevant to the project.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Contact me with the website link and the changes you need. I can review the request and confirm what is possible.

Do you offer video editing?

Yes. I take on video-editing work for digital content. Share the footage, intended platform and the result you want so the scope can be discussed.

How much does a website cost?

The listed website packages start at UGX 100,000. The final price depends on the agreed pages, features and project requirements. Any separate domain or third-party costs are discussed beforehand.

How do I start a project?

Contact me through WhatsApp or email with a short description of your idea. We can discuss the requirements, timeline and price before work begins.
